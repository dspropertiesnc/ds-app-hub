Screening Calculator

Applies SOP - Tenant Screening v1.3 with the D&S weighted score. Enter the Boom results; the calculator scores the file and builds the 1-page screening record.

1 · Application
Housing voucher (Section 8 / HAP)Income is measured against the tenant's portion.
2 · Income and credit (every adult)

Gross monthly income verified from actual documents (Step 5), not the amount the applicant reported. Credit score from each adult's Boom report; leave it blank for an adult with no score. The score uses the average of the adults who have one.

No adult has a credit score (thin file)Scored like 600 to 649 and sent for PM review.
3 · Liquid assets (only if short on income)

Don't count crypto, real estate equity, vehicles, or business ownership. Need the 2 most recent full statements per account.

4 · Rental history, evictions, collections, bankruptcy
The most recent filing was in the last 3 yearsDoes not meet criteria (a knockout), unless the applicant says it was DV-related; then it goes to PM review.
Bankruptcy filed or discharged in the last 5 yearsAny adult. Light weight (5% of the score); not a knockout.
5 · Knockouts (Does not meet criteria)
Eviction judgment within 7 years
Eviction filing at the applicant's current residenceThey'd be leaving a home where they've been filed on. Counts as a recent filing. If the applicant says it was DV-related (section 6), it goes to PM review instead.
Unpaid balance owed to a previous landlordIncluding landlord/rental collections.
Lifetime sex offender registration
Identity fraud, confirmed by the PM
6 · Needs PM judgment
Felony conviction within 7 years in a listed categoryViolent, sexual, arson/property damage, theft, fraud, drug manufacture or distribution. Record offense and dates.
Suspected identity fraud (not yet confirmed)
Applicant says a filing was related to domestic violence
International applicant (relocating from abroad)
Something the rules don't clearly cover
7 · Guarantee (only if income is under 3x)

Not needed: income meets 3x.

Guarantor lives in North Carolina

The guarantor also has to pass the same screening.

Before you rely on the score
Income in Boom is verified or re-verified from actual documents
Every adult has applied and paid the $70 fee
NC court search done for every adult
Identity verification passed (or re-verified)
8 · Decision and screening record

Attach the PDF to the application. Nothing is saved on the server.
