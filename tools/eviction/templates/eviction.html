Eviction Prep Packet

NC summary ejectment — courtroom quick-reference card + full prep binder

Upload what you have — PDF, Word, or photos/scans. The more of these, the more the packet fills itself in: lease (NCAR 410-T) + addenda · Complaint in Summary Ejectment (AOC-CVM-201) · Magistrate Summons (AOC-CVM-100, including the sheriff's return of service) · tenant ledger · Notice & Demand for Possession · SCRA declaration (AOC-G-250). Missing documents are OK — gaps get flagged rather than guessed.
